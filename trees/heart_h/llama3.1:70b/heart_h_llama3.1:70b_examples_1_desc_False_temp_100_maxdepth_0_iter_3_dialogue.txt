2. For patients with a lower maximum heart rate (<= 120 bpm), we further divide them based on Chest Pain Type. Those with asymptomatic chest pain are less likely to have heart disease.
3. Within the group of patients with non-asymptomatic chest pain, we consider Resting Blood Pressure as the next feature. Patients with high blood pressure (>130 mmHg) and ST depression induced by exercise are more likely to have heart disease.
4. For patients with a higher maximum heart rate (>120 bpm), we use Number of Major Vessels Colored by Fluoroscopy as the next feature. Those with at least one vessel colored are more likely to have heart disease.
5. Within the group of patients with no vessels colored, we consider Age as the final feature. Patients above 50 years old are more likely to have heart disease.

    nodes[6] = X["exang"] == 2

    if nodes[0]:
        if nodes[1]:
            if nodes[2]:

    else:
