- **Third Condition Check**: Only if both initial conditions are not satisfied does the function assess the WC_TA ratio. Regardless of whether this third condition is met, the prediction defaults to `0` (not bankrupt), as the prior ratios did not indicate distress.

- **Node Tracking**: Each condition evaluation is recorded in the `nodes` list, providing a clear audit trail of which criteria were checked and their outcomes. This is converted into binary form for clarity.

This structured approach ensures that each company is evaluated systematically against key financial metrics to determine its bankruptcy risk efficiently.
