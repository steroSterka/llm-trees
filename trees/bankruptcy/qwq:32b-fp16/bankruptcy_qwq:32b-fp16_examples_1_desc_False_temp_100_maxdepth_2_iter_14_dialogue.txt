This function evaluates whether a company is likely to face bankruptcy based on two financial ratios. The first check is on the ratio of equity to liabilities (`BVE/BVL`). If equity is less than or equal to liabilities (ratio ≤ 1), it indicates potential financial distress. The second check, only if the first condition is met, examines the company's profitability (`EBIT/TA`). If both conditions are true (low equity relative to liabilities and low profitability), the prediction is 1 (indicating bankruptcy risk); otherwise, it is 0. If the first condition isn't met, the prediction is 0 without further checks.
