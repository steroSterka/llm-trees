**Step-by-Step Explanation:**

1. **Initialization:** Create a list `nodes` with two boolean entries to store the results of the two conditions evaluated.

2. **First Condition (WC/TA):** Check if the Working Capital to Total Assets ratio (`WC/TA`) is less than or equal to 0. This indicates negative working capital, which is a strong indicator of financial distress. Store the result in `nodes[0]`.

3. **Second Condition (EBIT/TA):** Check if the Earnings Before Interest and Taxes to Total Assets ratio (`EBIT/TA`) is less than or equal to 0.05. A low EBIT/TA suggests low profitability, which is another risk factor. Store the result in `nodes[1]`.

4. **Decision Logic:**
   - If `nodes[0]` is `True` (negative working capital), the prediction is 1 (bankrupt).
   - If `nodes[0]` is `False` (positive working capital), evaluate `nodes[1]`:
     - If `nodes[1]` is `True` (low profitability), predict 1.
     - Otherwise, predict 0 (not bankrupt).

5. **Return:** The prediction and the evaluated conditions in `nodes`.

This model prioritizes working capital health first and then profitability as secondary indicators to assess bankruptcy risk.
