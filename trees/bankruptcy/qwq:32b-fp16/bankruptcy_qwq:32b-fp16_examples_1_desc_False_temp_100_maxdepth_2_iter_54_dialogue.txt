```
|--- WC/TA ≤ 0.1  
||--- Equity/Debt ≤ 0.5 → **Bankrupt**  
||--- Equity/Debt > 0.5 → **Not Bankrupt**  
|--- WC/TA > 0.1 → **Not Bankrupt**  
```

- **Working Capital/Total Assets (WC/TA):**
