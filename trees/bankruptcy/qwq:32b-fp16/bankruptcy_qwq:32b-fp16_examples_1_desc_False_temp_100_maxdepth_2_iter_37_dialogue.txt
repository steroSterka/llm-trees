def predict(X):
    nodes = [0, 0]
    
    # First node (root): Check if Working Capital to Total Assets ratio is low

If both conditions are true, the prediction is 1 (indicating potential bankruptcy). Otherwise, it returns 0. The nodes list tracks which conditions were met during evaluation.
