3. **Earnings Before Interest and Tax (EBIT)/Total Assets**: EBIT measures a company's profitability before considering interest and tax expenses. A low value (<= 0.05) suggests that the company is not generating enough profits to sustain itself, increasing its risk of bankruptcy.
