2. **Retained Earnings/Total Assets**: This ratio reflects a company's profitability and reinvestment policy. Higher retained earnings suggest that a company is profitable and has the capacity to finance its operations internally, reducing the risk of bankruptcy.

A low value here (< 0.10) may indicate insufficient internal financing for operations, again pointing towards bankruptcy. def predict(X: dict):
    """

             It should have the following keys:
             - 'WC/TA' (Working Capital/Total Assets)

           for the inner nodes of the decision tree.
    """

    # Initialize the list to store the truth values of the inner nodes
    nodes = 2 * [None]

    nodes[0] = X['WC/TA'] <= 0.20

    # Check if Retained Earnings/Total Assets is less than or equal to 0.10
    nodes[1] = X['RE/TA'] <= 0.10 if not nodes[0] else None
    
    # Prediction based on the decision tree
    if nodes[0]:
        prediction = 1  # Company likely went bankrupt due to low liquidity
    else:

            prediction = 1  # Company likely went bankrupt despite decent liquidity, due to poor profitability
        else:
