* If the degree of pain is more than mild, but there is no nasogastric reflux, the abdominal distension becomes a deciding factor. If it's more than slight, the lesion is likely to be surgical; otherwise, it's non-surgical.

```python
def predict(X: dict):

    nodes[0] = X["pain"] <= 4  # Degree of Pain (1-5)

    nodes[2] = X["abdominal_distension"] > 2  # Abdominal Distension (1-4)

    if nodes[0]:
        prediction = 0
    else:
        if nodes[1]:
            prediction = 1
        else:
            if nodes[2]:

                prediction = 0

```
